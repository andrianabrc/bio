I tuoi 5 prompt per foto prodotto

Quelli che uso per trasformare una foto qualunque in uno scatto da rivista. Per ognuno trovi la piattaforma giusta e un tip per usarlo al meglio.

Come usarli
1

Apri la piattaforma indicata e scegli il modello consigliato.

2

Incolla il prompt e sostituisci [PRODOTTO] con il tuo.

3

Per tenere il prodotto fedele carica una foto reference: Gemini e ChatGPT Images la usano per non alterarlo.

I 5 prompt

01

Packshot da catalogo

Lo scatto pulito su fondo neutro per la scheda prodotto.
High-end e-commerce packshot of [PRODOTTO], centered on a seamless warm ivory backdrop with a smooth gradient from light to soft shadow. Studio three point lighting: a large softbox key light from the upper left, a soft fill on the right, and a gentle rim light separating the product from the background. Crisp realistic contact shadow beneath it. Shot on a 100mm macro lens at f/8, tack sharp across the whole product, true to life colors and accurate material textures with every seam, reflection and finish visible. Clean, premium, catalog ready. Vertical 4:5 composition with generous negative space, high resolution.
Piattaforma: Gemini o ChatGPT Images
Tip: carica la foto reale del prodotto come reference per colori e dettagli fedeli.
02

Scena lifestyle

Il prodotto in un contesto reale, caldo e naturale.
Natural lifestyle photograph of [PRODOTTO] resting on a sunlit linen surface beside a window in a warm minimal home. Soft morning light raking from the left, gentle highlights and long soft shadows, warm palette of cream, oat and clay. A few tasteful out of focus props in the background such as a ceramic cup and a dried branch to suggest a lived in scene. Shot on an 85mm lens at f/2.0, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, the product in sharp focus. Editorial, airy, authentic, advertising quality. Vertical 4:5 framing, high resolution.
Piattaforma: ChatGPT Images o Gemini
Tip: allega la foto del prodotto così resta identico nella scena.
03

Hero editoriale

L'immagine da copertina, drammatica e di lusso.
Dramatic editorial hero shot of [PRODOTTO] as the single subject on a deep warm toned backdrop with a terracotta to espresso gradient. One strong directional light from the side sculpts the highlights and casts rich cinematic shadows, with a faint glossy reflection on the polished surface below. Luxury magazine aesthetic, fine material detail, a hint of atmospheric haze for depth, refined moody color grade. Shot on a 50mm lens with controlled contrast and museum quality lighting. Vertical 4:5 composition, ultra high resolution.
Piattaforma: Midjourney
Su Midjourney aggiungi: --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7
04

Flat lay stilizzato

Vista dall'alto con props, ottima per i caroselli.
Top down styled flat lay of [PRODOTTO] arranged with complementary props such as dried flowers, folded natural linen, textured handmade paper and a few smooth stones, on a warm travertine surface. Balanced, intentional composition with breathing room, soft diffused overhead daylight, gentle shadows, muted earthy palette of sand, ochre and stone. Shot straight down on a 50mm lens with even sharp focus, art directed and tactile. Square 1:1 composition, high resolution.
Piattaforma: Midjourney o ChatGPT Images
Su Midjourney aggiungi: --ar 1:1 --style raw --v 7
05

In mano / on model

Il prodotto indossato o tenuto, tattile e autentico.
Close up of [PRODOTTO] held naturally in a hand with realistic skin texture, fine detail and natural tone, a relaxed and elegant gesture. Soft directional window light, warm and flattering, neutral background gently thrown out of focus. Shot on an 85mm lens at f/2.2, shallow depth of field, the product in crisp tactile focus. Authentic, premium, advertising quality, warm color grade. Vertical 4:5 framing, high resolution.
Piattaforma: Gemini o ChatGPT Images
Tip: carica una foto del prodotto come reference per tenerlo coerente.
